## Which data do we process and why?

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Answering your question via the contact form | Name, email address, phone number (optional), message | Legitimate interest | Up to 2 years after the last contact |
| Sending the free e-book and related emails | Name, email address | Consent | Until you unsubscribe |
| Setting up and providing your membership | Name, email address, chosen membership, payment status | Performance of a contract | Duration of the membership + 2 years |

| Security and technical management of the website | IP address, browser and device data (server logs) | Legitimate interest | Limited period |

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do **not** receive or store full card or bank details.

## Who do we share your data with?

We never sell your data. We only share data with parties that help us provide our services (processors), and only to the extent necessary:

- **Stripe** — processing payments and subscriptions;
- **GoHighLevel (LeadConnector)** — customer management (CRM) and sending emails;
- **Vercel** — website hosting;
- **jsDelivr and unpkg** — content delivery networks used to load scripts for the website (your IP address is processed in the process).

Where required, we have concluded a data processing agreement with these parties. If you join our **Discord** community, Discord's own terms and privacy policy also apply.

Some of these parties are located outside the European Economic Area (EEA), for example in the United States. In that case we ensure appropriate safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Cookies

This website only uses **functional cookies** that are necessary for the website to work properly, such as a session cookie for secure administrator accounts. We currently do not use analytics or marketing cookies. If we do so in the future, we will ask for your consent in advance where required by law.

When you check out, you are redirected to Stripe's payment page. Stripe may set its own cookies there; see Stripe's privacy policy.

## Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss and unlawful use, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to systems.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your personal data;
- have incorrect data corrected;
- have your data erased;
- restrict processing;
- object to processing;
- data portability;
- withdraw your consent at any time.

Send your request to [support@efxo.com](mailto:support@efxo.com). We will respond within one month. Not happy with how we handle your data? You can lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (**Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens**).
