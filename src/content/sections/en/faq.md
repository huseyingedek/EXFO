  - question: "I have no experience. Can I start from scratch?"
    answer: "Absolutely. The programme starts with the very basics — what is a currency pair, what is a pip — and builds step by step to advanced topics. No prior knowledge is needed. We recommend practising everything on a demo account first."
  - question: "Do you give investment advice or trading signals?"
    answer: "No. EFXO Market Miners is an education platform. We do not give personal investment advice, buy or sell signals, and we do not manage money. All analysis is for educational purposes only. You always make your own decisions."
  - question: "Which markets do you cover?"
    answer: "We focus on forex (currency pairs such as EUR/USD and GBP/USD) and on CFDs on indices and commodities, such as the DAX, the S&P 500, gold and oil. The principles of analysis and risk management apply to all of these markets."
  - question: "Are CFDs suitable for everyone?"
    answer: "No. CFDs are complex, leveraged products, which means you can quickly lose more than you expect. Most retail investors lose money trading CFDs. Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and only once you fully understand how they work."
  - question: "Which broker should I use?"
    answer: "We don't recommend a specific broker. In the programme you do learn what to look for, such as a licence from the AFM or another European regulator, transparent costs and negative balance protection."
  - question: "How do I get access to Discord and the lessons?"
    answer: "After signing up you will receive a personal invitation to the Discord community and access to the video library by email. Live lessons and analysis channels are available straight away."
  - question: "Can I rewatch the lessons?"
    answer: "Yes. As long as your membership is active, you have unlimited access to the full video library on any device. New lessons are added automatically."
  - question: "Can I cancel my membership at any time?"
    answer: "Yes. A monthly membership can be cancelled every month; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. A yearly membership runs until the end of the paid year."
